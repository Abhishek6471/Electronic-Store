About ReTech Nepal

Our mission

ReTech Nepal helps people buy, sell and exchange quality-tested used and refurbished electronics, so good technology gets a second life and costs less.

How we work

Sellers list devices with an honest condition grade. Verified sellers get a badge, every listing shows its warranty and return terms, and buyers can compare devices before ordering.

About this prototype

This site is a frontend prototype. Payments, accounts, exchange valuations and the admin area are simulated in your browser and no real transactions happen.
