Frequently asked questions

How are products graded?

Listings use five grades: Like New, Excellent, Good, Fair and Refurbished. The seller describes any wear in the listing.

Is payment real?

Not in this prototype. Cash on Delivery is the default, and eSewa and Khalti are demo options that charge nothing.

How does delivery work?

Delivery costs Rs. 200 per order and is free over Rs. 50,000 by default. The admin can change these values.

Can I return a device?

Each product page states a 7-day return policy if the device does not match its listing.

How do I sell a device?

Create a Seller account, open Sell, fill in the listing form with images and submit it. An admin approves it before it appears in the shop.

How does exchange work?

Submit your old device on the Exchange page. Valuations are simulated in this prototype.

Are accounts secure?

No. Accounts are stored in your browser only. Real security needs a backend.
